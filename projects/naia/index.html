About

Submitted for the Mini Game Work Jam 2026: Conservación del Patrimonio Subacuático, organized by INAH (Instituto Nacional de Antropología e Historia) and Mermelada de Juegos.

The game is inspired by the discovery of Naia, a 12,000–13,000-year-old skeleton found in Hoyo Negro (Sac Actun cave system, Quintana Roo)—one of the oldest and best-preserved human skeletons in the Americas.

Features

  • Turn-Based Tactical Combat: Face off against prehistoric Pleistocene fauna and challenges in authentic 8-bit RPG style.
  • Atmospheric Top-Down Exploration: Explore submerged caverns, ancient jungles, and karst cenote systems.
  • Genuine Game Boy Hardware Compatibility: Runs on original Game Boy hardware via flash cart, emulators, or web browser.
